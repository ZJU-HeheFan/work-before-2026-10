# Homepage recent-work selection

The organization homepage highlights public work from **2025 and 2026**, ordered chronologically from newest to oldest. A group student or Hehe Fan must be the first author. Student identities are matched to the maintainer-provided group roster; public author order is checked against the paper or proceedings. Earlier work remains in the full index.

This is a curated selection of recent projects with public resources and visible community interest, rather than a complete publication list or a citation ranking. GitHub stars inform selection but do not measure scientific quality. Conference labels follow official proceedings where available. Repository buttons identify project repositories; they do not by themselves claim that an implementation has been released.

GitHub star snapshot: **10 October 2026**. Display order follows conference month for conference papers and the initial arXiv submission date for preprints. Works at the same conference are ordered alphabetically by project name. Stars do not determine display order.
